LUMA — Premium Streaming Platform

A cinematic streaming-platform UI/UX concept designed for Task 10 of the VOLTIX UI/UX internship.

LUMA helps viewers discover movies and series, explore content details, save a title for later, and continue watching previously started stories.

Main User Flow

1. Discover a title from the home screen or open Search.
2. Select the suggested search horizon to view the example results.
3. Open The Last Horizon and explore its details.
4. Select Add to Watchlist, then View Watchlist.
5. Remove the title from the Watchlist or use Undo to restore it.
6. Open Continue Watching and choose a story to resume.

Design Approach

• Cinematic dark theme: near-black surfaces keep attention on the artwork.
• Lime accent: highlights primary actions and viewing progress.
• Clear visual hierarchy: a large featured title leads into viewing history and recommendations.
• Readable typography: Inter is used throughout the experience.
• Consistent structure: reusable content cards and action components support a cohesive interface.
• Visible feedback: dedicated states communicate Watchlist additions, removals and unsuccessful searches.

Tools & Assets

• Figma: interface design, Auto Layout, reusable components and prototype connections.
• Inter: typography.
• Unsplash: photographic artwork used for the concept titles.

Project Scope

This repository documents a UI/UX design and interactive Figma prototype, not an implemented streaming application.

Search demonstrates predefined example states rather than a live catalogue. The Watchlist add/remove demonstration focuses on The Last Horizon. Player screens represent the viewing interface; they do not play video. Content titles, descriptions and credits are fictional concept content.
